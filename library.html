The Library

Every book recommended across this app, gathered in one place. The primary sources come first — the tradition in its own voice — then the modern guides. Almost all of the primary works are out of copyright, so most can be read (or downloaded) free.

⬇ Download as data

The list is generated straight from the reading sections of every topic, so it always stays in step with the app. "Copy the whole list" puts every Title — Author on your clipboard, ready to paste into a book downloader; "Download as data" gives the same list as a JSON file.
