## App Details
- App Name: Classical Architecture Study Guide (working title: "The Orders")
- Description: Standalone web app that teaches classical architecture — the five orders, temple anatomy, proportion, Vitruvian principles, styles across time, great buildings, and the architect's mind. Progressive lessons, flashcards, labeling games, and a Field Notebook of terms you have learned. Browser speech synthesis for narration. No paid APIs, no external dependencies at runtime.
- Target Audience: Hobbyist / enthusiast adult learner who wants to recognise and discuss classical buildings (US state capitols, European pre-WWI civic architecture).

## Technical Notes
- Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS — no framework, no build step, no npm.
- SVG line drawings generated in code (authentic teaching medium — classical treatises have always used engraved line art).
- LocalStorage for progress and Field Notebook.
- Hash-based routing (#/path/orders etc.)
- Can be opened directly from file:// OR deployed as static to Vercel — works both ways.
